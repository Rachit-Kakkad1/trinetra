# Brand Guidelines: The Neural Identity

talent-os is defined by a visual language of "Dark Luxury Minimalism." Every pixel should evoke a sense of future-tech intelligence and premium focus.

## 🎨 Color System

### Primary Palette
- **The Void:** `#07090F` (Base Background)
- **The Pulse:** `#FF8A1F` (Primary Action / Accents)
- **The Glow:** `#FFB347` (Secondary / Status / Warmth)

### Surface Palette
- **Glass:** `rgba(15,18,28,0.75)` (Dashboard Cards / Overlays)
- **Border:** `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` (Subtle Dividers)
- **Muted:** `#4B5563` (Secondary Typography)

## ✍️ Typography

### Headings: Space Grotesk
Used for major titles, headers, and OS-level identifiers. Evokes a sense of space-age engineering.

### Body: Inter
Used for UI elements, descriptions, and data points. Prioritizes legibility and modern cleanliness.

### System: JetBrains Mono
Used for terminal output, agent rationales, and technical feedback. Connects the user to the "raw code" of the OS.

## 🌊 Motion & Interaction

- **Duration:** Transitions should be snappy but fluid (200ms - 400ms).
- **Easing:** Use `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` for all cinematic transitions.
- **Elevation:** Use translucency and blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) rather than heavy shadows to create depth.

## 📸 Imagery & Icons

- **Icons:** Use **Lucide Icons** with a weight of `1.5px` or `2px`.
- **Glows:** Use radial gradients with low opacity to simulate ambient light from the OS.
- **Logo:** The talent-os logo should always be presented with a subtle outer glow when on dark backgrounds.
